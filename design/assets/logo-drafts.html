Implementation Notes

All drafts embed the actual animated.mp4 with autoplay, loop, and muted attributes. For production app icons (Draft F), use a static frame (hamster-still.png, included in assets). Every draft follows the FTP.TEST design system: Iosevka typeface, 3px borders, 14px radius, teal/pink/dark palette. The hamster's teal bike and pink bar tape naturally integrate with the brand colors.
