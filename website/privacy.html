Privacy Policy.

Effective: April 27, 2026
In Short

BetterFTP runs entirely on your phone. We collect nothing. No account, no servers, no analytics, no third-party tracking. Your test results stay on your device unless you choose to export them via the share sheet.

Permissions
  • Bluetooth — to connect to your indoor cycling trainer and heart-rate monitor over the Fitness Machine Service (FTMS) protocol.
  • Local file access — only when you tap Share, to write a .fit file you can export through your operating system's share sheet.
Platforms

Apple (App Store / TestFlight) and Google (Play) handle their own installation and crash reporting per their privacy policies. Aggregate, anonymized crash data may be shown to us in their developer consoles. We do not receive any identifiable user data.
